Transform one long-form field note or build log into a 5-slide 4:5 (1080x1350) Editorial Light-Table Carousel specification with a continuous cross-slide horizontal baseline rule.

[Paste your raw field note, measured result, verbatim quote, and honest limitation]

1. 5-Slide Copy & Visual Spec Table (Slide 01–05)

## Radical Honesty Gate (`DEC-01`)

- **ONE OUNCE TO KEEP:** Cap every carousel slide at 32 words and dedicate Slide 04 to `WHERE THIS BREAKS:`—readers trust the recommendation on Slide 05 because you showed the boundary on Slide 04.
- **WHERE THIS BREAKS:** Fails when a topic requires a 40-line code block that cannot be read comfortably at `1080x1350` on a phone screen (link to the GitHub repo instead).
