## Copy-Paste 4-Field Task Brief

```markdown
TASK:
Triage the unread messages below into a 4-bucket morning action brief so I can clear high-leverage replies in 15 minutes without inbox fatigue.

INPUT:
[Paste unread email subjects/snippets or connect via read-only inbox summary]

RULES:
1. Categorize every item into exactly one bucket:
   - [01 · REPLY TODAY]: Direct human questions, active client/family threads, time-sensitive commitments.
   - [02 · MONEY / OPS]: Invoices, contracts, receipts, tax/legal notices, infrastructure alerts.
   - [03 · READ LATER]: Worthwhile long-form dispatches or industry notes (summarize in 1 bullet each).
   - [04 · NOISE]: Cold pitches, automated marketing, unrequested follow-ups.
2. Draft concise, plain-spoken reply options for [01 · REPLY TODAY] items only.
3. Never send, archive, or delete messages autonomously—every outbound reply or state change requires explicit human approval.

OUTPUT:
1. 4-Bucket Triage Table (Sender, Subject, Bucket, 1-Line Why)
2. Draft Replies for Bucket 01 (awaiting human review)
```

---

## Radical Honesty Gate (`DEC-01`)

- **ONE OUNCE TO KEEP:** Separate classification from execution—let the agent sort the pile and draft the 3 real replies, but keep your own finger on the send button.
- **WHERE THIS BREAKS:** Requires an authorized email export or workspace connector; fails on image-only receipts or threads where critical context lives in an external attachment not passed in the input.
