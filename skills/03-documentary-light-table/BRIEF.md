## Copy-Paste 4-Field Task Brief

```markdown
TASK:
Seqeuence and caption a set of unposed documentary family or editorial photographs into a 5-frame story arc (Establish -> Rhythm -> Micro-Gesture -> Chaos/Humor -> Quiet Anchor).

INPUT:
[Paste your rough shoot notes, EXIF summary, verbatim quotes heard during the session, and 5–12 candidate frame descriptions]

RULES:
1. Prioritize genuine human micro-expressions, imperfect movement, and environmental context over stiff eye-contact portraits.
2. Pair at least one frame with an exact verbatim line spoken during the session (never invent or embellish quotes).
3. Keep captions under 18 words in Swiss contact-sheet style (Location · Focal Length · One honest observation).
4. Flag any frame that feels staged or over-directed for removal from the hero sequence.

OUTPUT:
1. 5-Frame Story Sequence (Frame #01 to #05 with role & caption)
2. Verbatim Anchor Pairing
3. Cut / Archive Recommendations (which frames belong in the client vault vs. the public contact sheet)
```

---

## Radical Honesty Gate (`DEC-01`)

- **ONE OUNCE TO KEEP:** A great documentary sequence needs only one wide establishing frame and one quiet closing anchor—everything in between should capture unprompted motion or spoken truth.
- **WHERE THIS BREAKS:** Fails when the underlying session was shot with rigid posing prompts; no sequencing brief can manufacture candid emotion after the shutter has fired.
